# Briefing S01-B13: sites, rooms, targets, and beacons beyond four

Track: theserver, Season S01. Repo `github.com/cyb3rgun/theserver`, `main` at the B12 handover or later. Implementer: CC. Architect: Mausi.

Everything the admin knows today is flat: devices with a room written into a text field. A venue is not flat. This pass gives the system its shape, because sessions, rankings, rights, prices and the franchise statement all hang from it, and every month it is missing costs a rebuild later. In the same pass the beacon side stops assuming four clusters.

## 1. Decisions

- **Three levels plus controllers.** A **site** is the venue: name, address, timezone, contact, notes. A **room** belongs to a site: name, age rating, WiFi channel, beacon multiplex plan (period in ms and number of slots), capacity, notes. A **target** is a device of kind target, belonging to a room, with its target type, its multiplex slot and a free position note. A **controller** is a device of kind controller, either assigned to a room or free for the whole site. D-066.
- **A session runs in a room.** Starting a session takes the room's targets automatically; single targets can still be excluded. The room's age rating is the session's age rating, and a scenario rated higher is refused with a translated message; the per device `min_age` from B12 stays as an override, and a device without a room keeps behaving as it does today. D-067.
- **Beacons are a list, not a rectangle.** A target type carries two to sixteen clusters, each with x and y in mm relative to the picture, plus the output channel of the target module that drives it. The calibration sent to a device becomes a list of points, `calib.pts` as id, x and y in canvas coordinates; `calib.rect` stays as the bounding box of those points so devices built against B12 keep working. The room's plan adds `beacon.period_ms`, `beacon.slots` and the target's own `beacon.slot`, sent as settings on approval, on change and on connect. Protocol section 8 records the keys; the firmware side follows in its own pass and already has room for eight channels in its mask. D-068.
- **Migration keeps what exists.** Every device that has a room text today lands in a site "Default" with a room named after that text, or in a room "Default"; nothing loses its history, and the old text field disappears from the pages. D-069.
- **The site carries the fields the franchise statement will need later**, stored and shown but not yet used for anything: licence id, franchise rate in percent, monthly threshold, currency, valid from. They are set by the manufacturer, read only for an operator role once roles exist. Writing them into the table now avoids rebuilding it when the money side arrives. D-070.
- **Tag `v0.5.0`** after this pass. D-071.

## 2. Tasks

1. `feat(store)`: migration with `sites`, `rooms`, `devices.room_id`, `devices.beacon_slot`, the site's licence fields, and the target type's cluster list gaining a channel per cluster; data migration per D-069; functions for the usual create, read, update, delete, plus "targets of a room" and "controllers of a site".
2. `feat(api)`: routes for sites and rooms, device assignment to a room, session creation from a room, `GET /api/v1/rooms/{id}/plan` returning the multiplex plan and the slots in use; the calibration endpoint returns points and rectangle; OpenAPI and its coverage test.
3. `feat(link)`: `calib.pts`, `beacon.period_ms`, `beacon.slots`, `beacon.slot` as settings to the device per D-068; the simulator logs what it received; tests for approval, change and reconnect.
4. `feat(admin)`: pages for sites and rooms, the room page listing its targets with type, slot and status and its controllers, assignment of devices to rooms, the session page choosing a room, the target type page drawing any number of clusters and taking the channel per cluster, the site page showing the licence fields. All texts in `en` and `de`.
5. `docs`: `docs/protocol.md` section 8 additions, `docs/concept.md` on the venue structure, D-066 to D-071, README, seasons, briefing verbatim; handover; push; tag `v0.5.0`.

## 3. Acceptance

A site with two rooms exists; room one holds three targets of two different target types, one of them with six clusters; each target receives its own point list and its own slot, and the simulator logs them; a session started on room one plays on all three; a scenario rated above the room's age rating is refused; devices from before the migration still work and appear in their rooms.

## 4. Report

`git log --oneline origin/main`, the tag, the migration result for the existing devices, the calibration lines for a four cluster and a six cluster target, screenshots of the site and room pages in both languages, and anything odd. A gap is a question, never a substitution.
