# S01-B13 handover

What the next session needs to continue without reading the log. A venue has a shape now. A site is the place, with its address, its timezone and the licence values the franchise statement will need (D-066, D-070); a room belongs to a site and carries the age of its players, the WiFi channel, the beacon multiplex plan and its capacity; a target is a device of kind target that stands in a room with a slot of that plan and a note about where it hangs; a controller belongs to a site and may belong to a room. A session runs in a room: it takes the approved targets of the room when it is created, a single one can be left out, and a scenario rated above the age of the room is refused with the room named (D-067). The beacon side stops assuming four clusters: a layout is a list of clusters with the output channel that drives each one, the device is told `calib.pts` with one point per cluster in canvas coordinates, and `calib.rect` stays as the box around them, beside `beacon.period_ms`, `beacon.slots` and `beacon.slot` (D-068). Everything that existed was moved into a site Default without losing its history (D-069). The close of the pass is tagged `v0.5.0`. `go vet ./...` and `go test ./...` are green on go1.27.1 in 17 packages with 458 test functions.

## Read this first

- **Migration 0011 does the move, and the SQL is the whole story.** It creates `sites` and `rooms`, adds `site_id`, `room_id`, `beacon_slot` and `position` to `devices` and `room_id` to `sessions`, then puts every device into `site-default` and into a room named after the text it carried. Room ids of the move are `room-` and six hex digits. The old `devices.room` column stays: it is what the device itself reported, and dropping a column in SQLite rewrites the table for nothing.
- **`site_id` on a device is not in the briefing's list, but follows from it.** A controller that is free for the whole site has to name that site without naming a room. It is recorded in D-066.
- **The calibration is a list, the rectangle is derived.** `store.TargetType.Calibration()` gives `Points` ordered by channel and, when they span an area, `Rect` as the bounding box with `HasRect`. `PointsValue()` writes `"id,x,y id,x,y ..."` and `Value()` the four corners as before; `Value()` is empty without a rectangle, and the link then does not send `calib.rect` at all.
- **A layout that names no channels is numbered.** `TargetType.numbered()` runs in create and update: if every cluster sits on channel 0 and there is more than one, they become 0, 1, 2 in the order they were written. An operator who does not care does not have to say, and one who does keeps what was typed.
- **One function sends everything a device is told.** `store.DeviceSetup` collects the calibration of its type and the plan of its room; `Setup.Settings()` is the ordered list of keys; `link.SendSetup` sends them with `set_config`. The API calls it after an approval, after a type change, after a placement and after a room change; the connection calls it in `setupOnConnect`.
- **Slots are unique per room and bounded by the plan.** `PlaceDevice` refuses a slot another target of the room holds and one above `beacon_slots` with `ErrSlotTaken`; `FreeSlots` is what the room page and the device page offer.
- **A session takes the room at creation, not at the start.** Agreed with the architect: an exclusion that the start undoes is not an exclusion. `RemoveSessionDevice` is the exclusion and is refused for a stopped session.

## Traps that survived

- **The room of a session is taken once.** A target hung after the session was created is not in it; add it on the sessions page. This is the price of an exclusion that holds.
- **`beacon_slot` 0 means none**, not slot zero. Slots count from 1, so a target without a slot is told nothing about one.
- **The licence fields are editable by every admin today.** Roles do not exist yet; D-070 says so plainly rather than pretending otherwise.
- **A room keeps its site.** `UpdateRoom` ignores a site in the body: a room that moves is a new room, because its devices and its sessions hang from it.
- **The old `devices.room` text is still written** by a device that reports it, and is no longer shown on any page. Read `room_id`, never `room`.

## Not done, open

- Nothing compares the class a device reports with the class of its type, and nothing warns when a scenario made for one type plays on another.
- A room plan is not checked against the hardware: nobody verifies that a period of 20 ms with 32 slots is something a target module can serve.
- The site page lists controllers but cannot put one into a room from there; that is done on the device page.
- The franchise fields are stored and shown and used by nothing, and no role keeps an operator out of them.
- Everything B11 and B12 left open stays open: one start per device, no retry of a refused start, readiness on load and not live, the orientation of a type without effect, and a deleted type leaving scenarios with an id that no longer resolves.
